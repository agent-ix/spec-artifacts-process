# Update Log

## History

* **2026-08-20** — **FR-003 CR-031**: `⚠️` is **retired** from the `Status` vocabulary (agent-ix/spec-artifacts-process#52, agent-ix/quire-rs#192). The contract admitted it at `column_patterns.Status` while `traceability.status` classed it as **nothing** — and the comment on the first even named the second as the source of truth while admitting a value it does not define. Not cosmetic: `class_of` returned `Unknown`, the rollup's only consumer asked `== Complete`, and the row fell out of the report — not backed, not a lie, listed nowhere. A `⚠️` row was exempt from the status-lie check **by construction**, which is how six FR-016 rows in `quire-cli` claimed evidence they did not have while `status_lies` read zero. **Retired rather than classed**, per unify-means-enforce: `🚧` already means pending and every measured `⚠️` cell reads `⚠️ Partial: …` or `⚠️ Pending`, the same meaning in a second form. **What the number counts:** rows under `## Test Case Summary`, column `Status`, in a `type: TestMatrix` document, across the 239 `~/dev` repositories — **20 rows in 5 repositories** (18 `⚠️`, 2 `🟡`); only **10 rows in 3 repositories** validate cleanly today and therefore gate the enforcing release. **The guard that should have caught this now does**: `test_column_vocabularies_have_one_source` asserted one direction only, and was confirmed to fail against the pre-change manifest (`admitted but not classed: ['⚠️']`) before the manifest was touched. A drift guard that cannot fail is the defect it exists to prevent. TC-008, TC-069.

* **2026-08-20** — **FR-007 CR-030**: `concolic-execution` drops `structured-input`, one release after CR-029 added it. It is what `grammar-based-fuzzing` is keyed on, so both methods were recommended on **identical evidence** and a reader had no basis to choose between them — while one is an escalation of last resort and the other is not. **[RAN]** `quoin advise` over quire-rs: **17 of 18** concolic recommendations came from `structured-input` and one from `reference-equivalence`. The count is not the argument; the argument is that a trigger shared with a cheaper method answering the same question distinguishes nothing. What survives are the values that separate the two: the wall a fuzzer cannot climb (`magic-value-comparison`), the mandate (`high-criticality`), the two proof obligations (`secret-dependent-branch`, `reference-equivalence`), and the plateau (`fault-detection-unmeasured` / `fault-detection-failed`). Found by running the advisor over a real corpus rather than by reading the entry.

* **2026-08-19** — **FR-007 CR-029**: `concolic-execution` becomes **reachable**, and the evidence-side characteristic is **renamed**. It was keyed on `path-sensitive` and `hard-to-reach-branch`, and nothing could produce either: both name the *implementation's* control flow, and a specification states what the system must do, never that a branch behind it is hard to reach. Measured across this catalog it was **the last of 33 methods no requirement could elicit** (agent-ix/quoin#128 took that count from 7 to 1).

  **The re-key follows how the technique is actually reached.** Nobody starts there — it path-explodes and it is slow. The industrial pattern is *hybrid fuzzing*: fuzz until the coverage curve flattens, hand the stuck branches to a solver, feed the solved inputs back as seeds (Driller 2016, then QSYM, SymCC, Fuzzolic). Four documented reasons people reach for it, every one observable: a stalled campaign (`fault-detection-unmeasured` / `fault-detection-failed`, from the evidence store), a safety standard mandating path coverage — DO-178C Level A MC/DC, ISO 26262 ASIL D, IEC 62304 Class C — (`high-criticality`, from the obligation's own value), constant-time code (`secret-dependent-branch`), and equivalence with a reference implementation (`reference-equivalence`). Plus `magic-value-comparison` — a checksum, CRC, HMAC or magic number, the classic wall a fuzzer cannot climb and a solver clears in one step — and `structured-input`. `haybale` joins the tooling: symbolic execution of LLVM IR written in Rust (crates.io 0.7.2), so it applies to anything rustc compiles.

  **`suite-quality-unknown` → `fault-detection-unmeasured`.** "Quality" over-claimed: a suite can be slow, flaky or unreadable and none of that is what this measures. "Suite" named the wrong subject: the fact is about *this obligation's* evidence, and the same suite may have a measured score for one obligation and none for another. Declared once and produced by nothing, so the rename was free — it will not be once it fires. Its counterpart is `fault-detection-failed`. **Deliberately not `surviving-mutants`**: that names mutation testing's artifact while `concolic-execution` reads the same signal and produces no mutants — CR-015's `evidence_kind` reasoning, one axis over.

  **Deliberately absent: a `fuzz-plateau` value**, the literal Driller trigger. Nothing records coverage over time and a proxy invented to fill the gap is the CR-014 failure — an open set whose membership had to be judged rather than read. **And what the entry still cannot say** is that it is an escalation of last resort: `advise` ranks by how many rules matched, so this now outranks `unit-testing` on a statement matching two triggers with nothing marking the difference. Filed as agent-ix/quire-rs#190; until it lands the guidance lives in quoin's `spec-evidence-analysis` and `spec-fuzz` skills, which is the skill-local-prose arrangement ADR-0011 moved away from — recorded as a debt rather than left to be rediscovered.

  AC-13/AC-14 and TC-067/TC-068 are **denylists, and that limit is deliberate**. Two general forms of AC-14 were written and both were worse than nothing: "does a declared tool name appear inside the value" passes `surviving-mutants` cleanly, because no tool is called *mutants*; reversed, it fires on `cross` in `crosshair` and `fault` in `fs-fault-injection`. A check that needs judgement is the failure this catalogue keeps citing, so the principle is stated in the CR and the tests guard the specific mistakes.

* **2026-08-19** — **FR-004 CR-028**: the traceability model declares **`trace_tags.implements`** (quire-rs FR-062) — the marker forms binding **production code** to the requirement it is about. `verifies` links an evidence symbol to a trace id; nothing linked a requirement to the code that implements it, so mutation scoping (quoin FR-039) had no file set to mutate. **[RAN]** across quire-rs's 52 functional requirements: **38 had at least one mutable target, 14 had none**, and the fourteen fail for one reason — every symbol verifying them lives in `tests/`. Reach correlated with **test placement**, not requirement quality (quire-rs CR-071). A separate list rather than a flag on `markers`, because `markers` mint evidence and these mint scope, and a shared list with a discriminator field puts one typo between the two; the engine keeps them apart structurally by binding `markers` to test/benchmark/fuzz symbols only and `implements` to functions and containers only, so a mis-declared form binds nothing rather than the wrong thing. A comment form (`/// Implements: FR-001, FR-002-AC-3`) rather than an attribute, since `#[implements("…")]` would need a proc macro in every consuming crate and the point is annotating production code that already exists. The literal `Implements:` keyword is the prose guard that the `*-comment-id` forms need a trailing delimiter for.

  **Declaring this block is what surfaced quire-rs CR-081.** v0.36.0 minted the relation and v0.37.0 carried it into `coverage --json`, but a module's declared forms were dropped between the manifest and the binding by two hand-maintained per-field functions (`merge_traceability` and `TraceabilityModel::is_empty`), so against v0.37.0 this declaration loads and mints **nothing** — the fourth instance of the engine-before-module ordering gap in this programme. Requires quire-rs **v0.38.0** / quire-cli **v0.26.0**. FR-004-AC-10, TC-066.

* **2026-08-18** — **CR-006**: new **FR-008** adds an optional **`Escape Cause`** column to the `SpecReview` findings table (agent-ix/spec-artifacts-process#36). A defect that is found got past everything meant to catch it; escape cause records **which layer let it through**, and nothing else in this module does. Severity says how urgently to look; a defect count says how many there were; neither says whether the specification, the evidence, or the implementation is leaking. Four values: `missing-requirement` (nobody wrote it), `wrong-requirement` (written, said the wrong thing), `correct-requirement-no-evidence` (right requirement, nothing verified it), `implementation-bug-despite-evidence` (right requirement, real evidence, wrong code). The fourth is the one to watch — the only cause that does not indict the spec, and the only one a green Test Matrix would have called healthy.

  **The first attempt put it on the wrong artifact, and the ticket pointed there.** #36 says the `Finding` archetype is a stub to be given a body contract and wired into the review skills. Both halves are false. **[RAN]** over `~/dev`: **169 `SpecReview` documents, 90 `Review` documents, and 2 typed `Finding` — both of which are mistyped analyses** (`AN-002 "Spec Integrity Analysis"`). Nothing has ever authored a real Finding. And `SpecReview` was created on 2026-06-20 *deliberately beside* the freeform `Review` archetype — "NOT overloading freeform `Review`, which is used ecosystem-wide with heterogeneous bodies — would break" — precisely so the analysis skills would not touch the `Review`/`Finding` container-and-child path. A contract on `Finding` would have attached the axis to a form nobody produces, and wiring the skills to it would have reversed a shipped decision. The ticket was written in an August research pass that did not know about the June one. CON-3 now states that the skills keep emitting SpecReview.

  **Optional, in two senses.** Declared in `optional_columns` (CR-023), so the ordered-subsequence rule keeps every existing four-column findings table valid — a required fifth column would have invalidated 169 documents the day it shipped. And optional because **not every finding is an escaped defect**: a completeness observation has no layer that leaked, and forcing a value would manufacture data.

  **The `Finding` id defect, fixed without giving it a contract.** `id_pattern: Finding-{next:03d}` minted `Finding-001`, which fails the archetype's own frontmatter schema `^[A-Z]{2,4}-[0-9]+$` — seven letters is not two-to-four — so **every id it produced was invalid** and a Finding authored the standard way could not validate. Now `FIND-{next:03d}`. Deliberately **not** `FND-`, which is the namespace of the findings *rows* inside a SpecReview: a Finding is a document and a child of `Review`, and sharing the prefix would read as unification and be a conflation. TC-063 asserts both properties — that the minted id validates, and that it does not fall in the row namespace. Matrix: TC-060..TC-064.

* **2026-08-17** — **CR-005**: two catalog entries added and the criticality question settled (agent-ix/spec-artifacts-process#43). quire-rs **v0.30.0** began reporting a declared method this catalog does not carry (FR-054-AC-11), and the first sweep — quire-rs' own 20 NFR `Measurement and Evaluation` tables, **55 rows, 17 distinct strings** — showed the `Method` column doing four jobs at once. Fifteen of the seventeen named something the catalog already had, under a different word: a **tool** (`Proptest`, `` `cargo-mutants` ``, `Criterion Benchmark`, `Fuzz Run`, `loom Exhaustive Interleaving` — and `deterministic-simulation` already lists loom in its own `tooling`), a **class synonym** (`Unit Test`, `Static Inspection`), or a **cadence** (`CI Gate` ×11, `Scheduled CI Gate` ×4, which say *when* a check runs and belong to the suite registry's schedule). FR-007 now carries the mapping table so a repository's sweep is a lookup rather than a judgement call, and **AC-10** stops this catalog making the same conflation the cells did.

  Two of the seventeen were real, and are added rather than forced into a near neighbour — a catalog whose nearest entry is wrong advises worse than one that admits the gap (**AC-9**, TC-056):

  - **`compile-time-check`** (Analysis / Compile) — the property is encoded so a violation does not build. `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` is not `static-quality`, which measures a weakness over source, and not `design-by-contract`, which checks an executable annotation wherever it runs. There is nothing to run.
  - **`dynamic-analysis-sanitizer`** (Analysis / Integration) — TSAN, ASAN, Miri. Not `static-quality` (it executes), not `fuzzing` (it generates no input), not `fault-injection` (it induces nothing). The catalog had **no dynamic-analysis entry at all**, which is a real hole in a 29119-4-shaped registry rather than a quire-rs-specific need.

  **Criticality: already answered upstream.** This module declares no `criticality_column`, so every obligation the ecosystem derives carries none — and quoin's auditor hardcoded a `P0` multiplicity rule against it, a rule that could never fire. quire-rs [FR-053](ix://agent-ix/quire-rs/FR-053) already specifies the arrangement ("criticality is genuinely optional today … declaring one is a module's choice"), so the correction belonged in quoin rather than here. What is worth recording is *why* the column cannot simply be added: the ISO acceptance-criteria contract asserts exactly `ID | Criteria | Verification` with no `optional_columns`, and the engine's column assert is exact equality, so a fourth column fails validation — adding criticality is a `spec-artifacts-iso` change first, not an authoring habit. `Priority` exists only on the Test Matrix row, where it rates the **test** rather than the requirement. FR-007 records this and states that until the contract changes a consumer must not assume any obligation carries a criticality. Matrix: TC-056, TC-057.

* **2026-08-17** — **FR-007** (new FR): the **verification-method catalog content** — 31 methods as module data conforming to quire-rs FR-054 (agent-ix/spec-artifacts-process#35, epic agent-ix/quire-rs#81 P1). Choosing how a requirement gets verified is the core of a testing plan, and that knowledge was prose in three disconnected places: IADT lived in two spec-artifacts-iso lint rules, techniques in `traceability.vocabularies.test_type`, and quoin's `spec-evidence-analysis` skill assigned a method per requirement from a **skill-local table that no manifest declared and no code read**. The seed set is the **full sweep**, not a starter subset — 21 Test, 7 Analysis, 1 Inspection, 2 Demonstration — because a catalog shipping half the techniques teaches the advisor the other half do not exist, and an absent entry reads as "not applicable" rather than "not yet written". `evidence_kind` is drawn from the declared `test_type` vocabulary and is **method-class-shaped, never per-tool**: the tool lives in the suite registry's own `tool` column, so semgrep, an SBOM generator and a DAST scanner need no vocabulary entries of their own — without that split every adapter quoin adds would push a new term into a vocabulary three other things already read. Applicability rules use this module's own axes (`property_shapes`, `characteristics`, `object_types`), which the engine stores and never interprets; they are what make the interesting advice reachable — an `attack_surface` object suggests DAST, temporal phrasing suggests runtime monitoring or model checking, a reliability NFR suggests fault injection, a concurrency shape suggests deterministic simulation. None of those are reachable from `Verification: Test` defaulted by habit.

  **The reconciliation #35 asked for is partial, and deliberately so.** The catalog single-sources the *method* axis for consumers, through quire-rs's derived `verification_method` / `verification_class` lookups. The *kind* axis is still three literal lists — catalog, Test Matrix `Type`, suite registry `Evidence Kind` — because `from_vocabulary` was deferred (quire-rs#146); TC-050 is what keeps them honest until it lands.

  **The FR-035 gate earned itself again.** Declaring the block failed this repo's own suite on `Additional properties are not allowed ('verification_catalog' was unexpected)` — the key the engine accepts and the contract had never heard of. Before iso CR-002 that check skipped in silence. Fixed upstream in spec-artifacts-iso **v0.11.0** (FR-001 CR-003) and consumed here. Matrix: TC-048..TC-054. Closes agent-ix/spec-artifacts-process#35.

* **2026-08-17** — **FR-006** (new FR): the **evidence-layer archetypes**, `SuiteRegistry` and `Inspections` — the authored half of the ADR-0011 evidence store (agent-ix/spec-artifacts-process#34, epic agent-ix/quire-rs#81 P1). The store splits by who writes it: authored → markdown validated as a document, machine-transcribed → JSON that is typeless and corpus-invisible by design. The JSON half needs no archetype and gets none; the markdown half needs both, because a suite registry nothing validates is a list of commands nobody checks. `SUITE-N` is a **structured, doc-scoped, never-renumbered** id rather than a kebab slug — everything joins on it (run directories, bindings, freshness) and a policy-immutable semantic string is exactly what produced the 1,014 dead trace tags (quire-rs#72); the suite's name and command live in their own columns, where they can change without breaking a join. `Evidence Kind` draws from the **same** declared `test_type` vocabulary as the Test Matrix `Type` column and the obligation record's method (CR-015: one vocabulary, three uses), with the tool in its own column so a tool-specific report — semgrep, SARIF, an SBOM — needs no vocabulary entry of its own. That vocabulary is **restated rather than referenced**, a known debt: `column_choices` takes a literal list and `from_vocabulary` was deliberately deferred (quire-rs#146), so TC-042 is what keeps the copies honest until it lands.

  **Both documents live under `spec/`, which corrects a premise in agent-ix/quoin#79.** That design places `evidence/` at the repository root on the grounds that "corpus membership is type-driven post-quire-rs#73, so quire validates them wherever they live". **[RAN]** it: quire-rs CR-045 bounds the document walk to `<scope>/spec`, and #73 made membership type-driven *within* the walked root, not everywhere on disk. A typed, well-formed `evidence/suites.md` at the repository root minted **nothing** and was reported nowhere; the identical file at `spec/evidence/suites.md` was walked, validated, minted its ids and had its references reconciled. A registry that looks authoritative while being checked by nothing is the "green matrix over dead links" failure reproduced inside the store built to prevent it. Filed on quoin#79 rather than silently overridden.

  Two smaller calls. Neither archetype declares `allowed_links`: these documents are joined by declared **trace** references, not edge verbs, and declaring `references` would have added two more `UnknownEdgeType` warnings to every repo on this module for nothing — measured at zero evidence-layer mentions for a non-adopting repo (TC-047). And the FR-003 scope guard was widened by two explicit entries rather than loosened, so a contract arriving by accident still costs a deliberate line. Matrix: TC-040..TC-047. Closes agent-ix/spec-artifacts-process#34.
