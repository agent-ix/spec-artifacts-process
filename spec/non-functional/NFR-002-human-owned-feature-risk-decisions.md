# NFR-002: Feature risk judgements remain human-owned and non-computed

## Statement

The module SHALL record feature risk ratings and supporting failure-mode and detection information as supplied by a named person, without deriving or assigning ratings, scores, product criticality, software safety classes or risk-acceptability decisions.

The module SHALL NOT derive ratings from code metrics, test coverage or test results.

The signature-verification workflow SHALL report whether a named person's signed acceptance record satisfies the stated signature conditions.

The signature-verification workflow SHALL not choose whether the person takes the risk.

The person who owns the intended use makes that decision.

## Scope

- Applies to `IntendedUse` and `FeatureRiskRating` documents and validation rules defined by FR-024 through FR-027.
- Adopters may use the recorded information with other evidence to make their own TCL, TQL or T-class determination for their intended use.

## Rationale

Risk decisions belong to the person responsible for the intended use. Keeping the information and the decision separate lets adopters make their own assessment without treating a tool-generated value as an approval.

## Measurement and Evaluation

| Metric | Target | Threshold | Method |
|--------|--------|-----------|--------|
| Rating, score, class or risk-acceptability values generated by module rules or defaults | 0 | 0 | Inspection |
| Feature ratings traceable to a named human and intended use | 100% | 100% | Test |
| Signature-acceptance results matching the stated verification conditions | 100% | 100% | Test |

## Verification

Review the schema and rule definitions for generated rating values, and validate fixtures with a named rater and intended-use context.
