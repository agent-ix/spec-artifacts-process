# NFR-003: Feature risk ratings and decisions are not computed

## Statement

The module SHALL NOT derive or assign feature-risk ratings.

The module SHALL NOT derive or assign risk scores or probabilities.

The module SHALL NOT derive or assign product-criticality values.

The module SHALL NOT assign software-safety classes.

The module SHALL NOT derive risk-acceptability decisions.

The module SHALL NOT derive ratings from code metrics.

The module SHALL NOT derive ratings from test coverage.

The module SHALL NOT derive ratings from test results.

## Scope

- Applies to schemas, mappings and validation rules for `IntendedUse` and
  `FeatureRiskRating` defined by FR-024 through FR-026.
- Adopters may use the recorded information with other evidence to make their
  own TCL, TQL or T-class determination for their intended use.

## Rationale

Risk decisions belong to the person responsible for the intended use. Keeping
the information separate from computed values lets adopters make their own
assessment without treating a tool-generated value as an approval.

## Measurement and Evaluation

| Metric | Target | Threshold | Method |
|--------|--------|-----------|--------|
| Rating, score, class or risk-acceptability values generated by module schemas, mappings or rules | 0 | 0 | Test |

## Verification

Validate the emitted rating schemas and representative fixtures to confirm that
the module defines no computed score, class or acceptability value.
